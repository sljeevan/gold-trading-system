## 3. Macro Regime Analysis: Why the Two Halves Differ

Testing across a full year exposes two radically different Gold market regimes:

### 1. The Winter/Spring Chop Phase (Nov 2025 – Apr 2026)
- **Market State**: Spot Gold spent 5 months trapped in a tight $150 trading range without establishing a clear macro direction.
- **Impact on 10-Bar Donchian**: Fast 10-bar touch triggers repeatedly clipped channel edges only to snap back into range mean-reversion, causing lower win rates (31%–42%) and monthly drawdowns (e.g. November -$544.96 on 0.02 lots).
- **The January Exception**: January 2026 saw a massive seasonal directional breakout (+52.6% WR, **+$776.08** on 0.02 lots).

### 2. The Summer/Fall Expansion Phase (May 2026 – Oct 2026)
- **Market State**: Multi-week directional legs (record gold highs, rate cut catalysts).
- **Impact on 10-Bar Donchian**: Outstanding performance! All 6 consecutive months were net profitable, generating **+$1,286.28** on 0.02 lots with a 52.0% win rate.

## 4. Why Capital Management Rules Are Essential

This 1-year test clearly proves **why the Compounding Ladder Manager was built with capital protections**:
1. When lot size is fixed and completely unmanaged through chop, a 5-month consolidation will erode profits before the next trend leg arrives.
2. The **2-Loss Circuit Breaker** (which cuts risk by 50% during chop) and **Progressive Ratchet Floors** (which lock in profits at $350 and $700) protect equity during difficult range months like November and April, preserving the capital to capture the massive expansions like January, May, June, and August.
